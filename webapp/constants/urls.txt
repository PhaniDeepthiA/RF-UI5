

① Fetch Inbound Delivery Items

 _fetchInboundDelivery

GET
https://my430184.s4hana.cloud.sap/sap/opu/odata/sap/API_INBOUND_DELIVERY_SRV;v=0002
/A_InbDeliveryHeader('180000088')/to_DeliveryDocumentItem

✔ Expected:
	•	Material
	•	Plant
	•	StorageLocation
	•	DeliveryDocumentItem
	•	ReferenceSDDocument (PO)
	•	DeliveryDocumentItemCategory

If this fails → nothing else matters

https://my430184.s4hana.cloud.sap/sap/opu/odata/sap/API_INBOUND_DELIVERY_SRV;v=0002

  DeliveryDocument='180000088',

⑤ Fetch Handling Units for IBD

⚠️ This is the critical RF call

GET
https://my430184.s4hana.cloud.sap/sap/opu/odata4/sap/api_handlingunit
/srvd_a2x/sap/handlingunit/0001/HandlingUnit
?$filter=HandlingUnitReferenceDocument eq '180000088'
and Warehouse eq 'PU01'
&$expand=_HandlingUnitItem

✔ Expected:
	•	Multiple HUs for partial GR
	•	CreationDateTime present
	•	_HandlingUnitItem populated

❌ If empty:
	•	HU was created before GR
	•	or reference is not IBD
	•	or HU already consumed

⑥ Fetch HU directly (debug / fallback)

👉 _fetchHUDetails

GET
https://my430184.s4hana.cloud.sap/sap/opu/odata4/sap/api_handlingunit
/srvd_a2x/sap/handlingunit/0001/HandlingUnit(
  HandlingUnitExternalID='300000269',
  Warehouse='PU01'
)
?$expand=_HandlingUnitItem

✔ Use this to prove HU exists
✔ If this works but step ⑤ doesn’t → reference mismatch confirmed
